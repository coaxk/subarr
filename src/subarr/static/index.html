          What is Automation?
          
        
          
          Subarr can run on autopilot. Instead of you clicking through Coverage every
          day and queuing missing-subtitle episodes by hand, two background jobs do
          it for you:
        
          
        
            
          
          1. 
            Probe walk — looks inside your video files
            (using ffprobe) to detect embedded subtitles and the audio language.
            This is what lets subarr know that S01E03.mkv already has
            a usable English sub baked in, so it can skip it.
          
            2. 
          
          2. 
            Scheduled coverage walk — on a schedule you choose
            (e.g. nightly at 3am), subarr re-pulls Bazarr's wanted list, applies
            your auto-queue rules, and either shows you a confirm list, queues
            matches automatically, or just displays the gap — your call.
          
            3. 
        
          
        
          
          Recommended first-time setup: kick off a probe walk
          on each library root (TV, Movies) so Coverage
          becomes accurate. Then enable the schedule in
          manual_confirm mode — you'll get a Pending Approvals list
          each morning to review before anything submits. Once you trust it,
          switch to auto_rules.
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Probe walk (ffprobe)
          
        
          
          Deep-scans a folder, opens each video with ffprobe, and records which
          subtitle/audio tracks are embedded. Results land in the cache that
          Coverage and the Library tab read — rows with a usable English embedded
          sub get demoted to "ok" automatically, so they stop nagging you.
        
          
        
          
          How to use: type a canonical path (relative to your
          library root — see Settings → Path format reference) and hit
          Start walk. Big libraries take a while; you can keep using
          subarr while it runs. Re-run any time you add new media or want fresh
          data.
        
          
        
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
          Tip: leave the field blank to walk every library root that subarr knows
          about. Recent walks appear below — click one to see per-file progress.
        
          
        
          
      
          
      
          
        
          Schedule & Auto-queue
          
        
          
          Runs a background coverage walk on a recurring schedule. Each run
          re-pulls Bazarr's wanted list, applies your auto-queue rules below, and
          — depending on Mode — either shows you the gap, queues a
          confirmation list, or submits scans straight to subgen. Off by default;
          flip Enabled when you're ready.
        
          
        
          
          Modes explained:
        
          
        
            
          
          • dashboard — runs the walk for fresh stats only. Nothing gets queued. Safe starting point.
            • 
          
          • manual_confirm — runs the walk + builds a list of candidates that match your rules. They show up under Pending approvals below for you to approve/reject in bulk.
            • 
          
          • auto_rules — full autopilot. Matching candidates get submitted to subgen automatically, every run. Switch on once you trust the rules.

          Rules
          
        
          
          Filters that decide which missing-subtitle candidates the scheduler
          picks up. Min score is Bazarr's wanted-row score (higher =
          more confident the episode actually needs subs). Skip stale
          ignores rows where a .srt file already exists on disk (catches Bazarr
          listings that haven't refreshed yet). Skip embedded EN uses
          the probe cache to skip files that already have a usable English sub
          baked in. Allow/Deny languages let you target only the languages you
          care about.
